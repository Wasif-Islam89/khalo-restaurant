Official KhaLo Food Services Policy Applies to all KhaLo web apps, meal finder tools & ordering services in Pakistan.

01 Information We Collect

When you explore dishes, match cravings on Find My Meal, or place an order for hot handis and burgers through KhaLo, we collect necessary information to fulfill your meal seamlessly:

  • check_circle Account & Contact Details: Name, mobile phone number (for rider dispatch and order verification via SMS/WhatsApp), and email address.
  • check_circle Delivery Address & Neighborhood: Building, street, gate code, and neighborhood choice (such as Clifton, DHA, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, PECHS, Nazimabad).
  • check_circle Meal Preferences & Cravings: Liked dishes in Favorites, spice level preferences, budget choices, and dietary notes added to your order cart.

02 How We Use Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers. Your information is used strictly to power your dining experience:

  • soup_kitchen Fulfilling kitchen orders, cooking fresh handi biryanis, and timing meals to arrive piping hot.
  • moped Assigning the nearest verified delivery rider (such as Tariq Khan) and calculating accurate live arrival estimates.
  • local_offer Applying authorized discount vouchers (e.g., KHALO50) and verifying loyalty perks.
No Unsolicited Spam Guarantee

KhaLo will never spam your phone. SMS notifications are strictly reserved for real-time order updates (Order Confirmed, Out for Delivery, Rider at Gate).

03 Karachi Delivery Tracking & Location Data

To ensure your food is delivered fresh within 25–35 minutes, KhaLo utilizes local delivery hub routing across Karachi:

Your neighborhood choice (saved in your local browser preferences) is used to display partner cloud kitchens within your immediate radius. We do not track your background GPS coordinates when the KhaLo website is closed.

04 Rider & Kitchen Information Sharing

To complete your order, limited necessary information is shared only with certified fulfillment partners:

  • check_circle The Assigned Kitchen: Receives dish titles, special cooking instructions (e.g., "extra raita, mild spice"), and order ticket number. They do not receive your payment card numbers.
  • check_circle The Delivery Rider: Receives your delivery drop-off point and contact number solely to call upon arrival at your gate or building lobby.

05 Payment Safety & Cash on Delivery

Bank-Grade Encryption

Digital transactions are handled through SBP-compliant payment gateways with 256-bit SSL encryption. KhaLo servers never store your raw CVV or card PIN numbers.
